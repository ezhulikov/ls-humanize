\[Banned Constructions\]

Trailing comma-negation contrast: "..., not a/an/the/one ___" (or "not extra/not on/not billed as" etc.) tacked onto the end of a sentence to imply a contrast, e.g. "arrived as one complete set, not a filing receipt with an upsell screen wedged in behind it" or "earns the top spot for what happens after checkout, not the number on the landing page." Rewrite so the sentence stands on its own without a bolted-on negated alternative at the end — move the comparison earlier in the sentence (e.g. "instead of," "more than," "well beyond," "something most competitors do") or drop the negated half entirely if it adds nothing factual.
